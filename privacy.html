Privacy Policy

OCV Pipeline Publisher — internal publishing tool
Effective 23 September 2026

Who we are

Becam Industries LLC, doing business as Odyssey Camper Vans, based in Kalamazoo, Michigan, United States.

This policy covers OCV Pipeline Publisher, a Meta application we built and operate for our own use. It does not cover any other website, product or service.

What this application is

OCV Pipeline Publisher is a private, internal tool. It uploads and publishes our own video content to social media accounts that we own and administer — the OCVTravel Facebook Page and the associated Instagram account — and reads back the performance statistics for those posts.

It is not a product offered to the public. There is no sign-up, no sign-in and no user account. Nobody outside our organisation uses it, connects an account to it, or is asked for any information by it.

Information the application accesses

Using access tokens issued to us by Meta for our own accounts, the application accesses:

  • Our own Facebook Page and its linked Instagram account — the account name and identifier needed to publish to them.
  • Video files we have produced ourselves, uploaded from our own computer to our own accounts.
  • Captions, titles, location tags and comments that we write, for posts we publish.
  • Aggregate performance statistics for our own posts — view counts, reach and similar figures that Meta reports to us as the owner of the content.

Information the application stores

The application runs on our own computer. Everything it stores is held locally on that machine and is not transmitted to any server we operate:

  • Access tokens for our own Meta accounts, stored in a permission-restricted file.
  • Identifiers for posts we have published, so the same video is not posted twice.
  • Location identifiers for the towns we have filmed, so a place tag is chosen once per town.
  • The captions and text we wrote for our own posts.

We operate no public server, database, or hosted service in connection with this application.

Information we do not collect

The application does not collect, request, receive, store or process personal information about any other person. Specifically, it does not collect:

  • Names, profile information, email addresses or contact details of anyone who views, likes, comments on or shares our posts.
  • Login credentials belonging to anyone other than ourselves.
  • Location data about any individual.
  • Payment or financial information of any kind.
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers, cookies or tracking data. The application has no web interface and sets no cookies.

Aggregate statistics we read about our own posts are counts and totals. They do not identify individual viewers to us.

How information is used

Solely to publish our own video content to our own social media accounts, and to review how that content performed so we can decide what to make next. Nothing more.

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade or share any information with third parties.

The application communicates only with Meta's own Graph API in order to publish to our accounts. Meta's handling of that data is governed by Meta's Privacy Policy, not by this one.

We may disclose information if required to do so by law.

Data retention and deletion

Because the application holds no personal information about anyone other than ourselves, there is ordinarily nothing for a third party to request the deletion of.

Our own stored tokens and publishing records are kept only while they are useful and can be deleted by us at any time by removing the local files that hold them.

If you believe we hold information about you and would like it removed, or you would like to ask what we hold, write to info@odysseycampervans.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request. We will respond within 30 days, confirm what if anything we hold, and delete it on request.

To remove this application's access to our Meta accounts entirely, we revoke its permissions from within our own Meta account settings, which ends its access immediately.

Security

Access tokens are stored in a permission-restricted file on a single computer under our control. We do not transmit them to any third party and we do not store them on any server.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children

This application is not directed at children, is not used by anyone other than us, and knowingly collects no information from anyone, children included.

Changes to this policy

If what the application does changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date at the top. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect.
